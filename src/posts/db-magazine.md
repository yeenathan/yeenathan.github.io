A print magazine on the architecture of the Dragon Ball universe.

With print in mind, the magazine design takes inspiration from Akira Toriyama's _Dragon Ball_ manga series. Proper use of grids, styles, swatches, etc. ensures visual consistency and coherence.

Assets are borrowed from online _Dragon Ball_ manga panels and/or recreated in Illustrator.
